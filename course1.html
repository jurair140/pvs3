About This Course

The B.Voc in Medical Laboratory Technology is a comprehensive 3-year undergraduate program designed to equip students with essential skills in clinical diagnostics and laboratory procedures. This course blends theoretical knowledge with hands-on training in disciplines such as pathology, microbiology, hematology, and biochemistry.

Through practical sessions and internship opportunities, students gain expertise in sample collection, testing, analysis, and reporting. The curriculum is aligned with industry standards and prepares graduates for careers in hospitals, diagnostic labs, research facilities, and public health organizations.

Emphasis is placed on laboratory safety, biomedical waste management, and usage of modern diagnostic equipment. Students are trained to handle real-world medical cases, maintain quality control, and follow ethical practices in healthcare. Graduates of this program will be eligible for advanced studies, government licensing exams, and a variety of healthcare-related roles both in India and abroad.
