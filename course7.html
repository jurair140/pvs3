About This Course

Medical Coding is a 6-month professional program that prepares students for a booming career in the healthcare industry. It focuses on converting medical diagnoses, procedures, and services into universal codes for billing and documentation.

The course is designed to help learners become proficient in ICD-10, CPT, and HCPCS coding systems with hands-on practice in billing software and claim processing.

With growing demand in hospitals, insurance firms, and outsourcing companies, this job-oriented course opens doors to global career opportunities. Students also receive placement assistance and mock interviews as part of the training.
